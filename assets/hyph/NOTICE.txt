Lists of patterns for dividing words at the ends of lines
==========================================================

Reiks Document reads the files in this folder as data. They are not part of the program, they
were not written for it, and each keeps the licence its authors gave it. All of them were taken,
unchanged, from the TeX hyphenation project (the hyph-utf8 package):

    https://github.com/hyphenation/tex-hyphen
    http://www.hyphenation.org/tex


English, American spelling
--------------------------

    hyph-en-us.tex       the file of the authors, complete and unchanged, with its notices
    hyph-en-us.pat.txt   the patterns of that file as a plain list, as the project gives them out
    hyph-en-us.hyp.txt   the words divided by hand of that file, as a plain list

Copyright (C) 1990, 2004, 2005 Gerard D.C. Kuiken

    Copying and distribution of this file, with or without modification,
    are permitted in any medium without royalty provided the copyright
    notice and this notice are preserved.

The file holds the patterns of Donald E. Knuth's hyphen.tex (the Plain TeX hyphenation tables,
made with Frank M. Liang's method) together with Dr. Kuiken's additions; hyph-en-us.tex says
which lines are whose.

    hyph-sv.pat.txt      the patterns of that file as a plain list, as the project gives them out

hyph-sv.pat.txt is not the author's own file: it is the list of patterns taken out of
hyph-sv.tex by the TeX hyphenation project, under another name, and nothing in it was changed
for Reiks Document. The author's own file lies beside it.


languages.txt in this folder belongs to Reiks Document. It names the languages and how many
letters at least stand before and behind a hyphen (the values the files above give for
typesetting).
